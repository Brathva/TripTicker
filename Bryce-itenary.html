🌄 Day 1 — Vegas to Bryce Canyon

  • Leave Paris Las Vegas around 8–10 AM
  • Optional coffee stop at St. George ☕
  • Quick scenic stop at Red Canyon 📸
  • Check in at Clear Sky Resorts Bryce Canyon 🏕️
  • Sunset at Bryce Canyon 🌅
  • Late-night stargazing 🌌✨
⭐ Best Stargazing: 10 PM – 2 AM

🌌 Bryce Stargazing Experience

  • Milky Way visibility is incredible here ✨
  • Bring tripod + warm jacket 🧥
  • Use night mode for photography 📷
  • Minimal light pollution 🌠
🌙 Bryce Canyon is one of the best dark-sky parks in the USA.

🥾 Day 2 — Bryce to Mesquite

  • Sunrise at Sunrise Point 🌞
  • Queen’s Garden + Navajo Loop 🥾
  • Explore hoodoos and viewpoints 🧡
  • Drive to Mesquite 🚗
  • Relax at Holiday Inn Resort 🏨
🚗 Approx drive: 4.5–5 hrs with scenic stops

🔥 Day 3 — Valley of Fire & Flight

  • Leave early around 6:30–7 AM ⏰
  • Visit Elephant Rock 🐘
  • Explore Fire Wave 🌊🔥
  • Rainbow Vista & Seven Sisters 🌈
  • Drive back to airport ✈️
💧 Carry LOTS of water. Desert heat gets intense!
Made for an epic Southwest USA adventure 🌵🚘✨
